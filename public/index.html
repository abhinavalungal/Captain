Meet K.R.1.S

Ask your fleet anything. Get the answer, not an essay.

K.R.1.S (say it “Kris”) is built for maritime emissions and marine technology — EU ETS, FuelEU Maritime, CII, EEXI and your fleet’s fuel and carbon intensity. It picks the fastest exact route to each answer, and shows it visually when a picture reads faster.

  1. 1

    Instant lane ~1 ms

    Deadlines, unit conversions (knots, nautical miles, tonnes, kWh) and number comparisons are worked out exactly on the server — typos and all.

  2. 2

    Your records SQL

    Fuel, CO₂, GHG intensity and FuelEU balance questions become one parameterised query against your synced data, scoped to your department, with the source beside every figure.

  3. 3

    N.A.V 3.8b streamed

    Regulation questions — EU ETS, FuelEU Maritime, CII, EEXI — and everything else are written by the conversation model and streamed as they are written, behind a guard that stops any invented vessel figure.

Developers

Add K.R.1.S to perform.geoserves.com.

Paste this before </body>. The widget works out its own API address from where the script was loaded, so nothing else on your page needs configuring.

  1. Set KRIS_ALLOW_ORIGIN to https://perform.geoserves.com so the browser may call K.R.1.S from your domain.
  2. Replace verifyToken() in src/httpHandler.js with your real session check and remove KRIS_DEV_SESSION. getToken should return the user’s real session token.
  3. On vessel pages, call KRIS.setContext({ vesselId, vesselName }) when the page loads.
Prototype sign-in trusts whatever department the browser claims. It exists so this page can be demonstrated before real authentication is wired, and must be off on any site real users can reach.
